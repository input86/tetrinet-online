Player 1 is you. Players 2–6 are computer opponents that play by the same rules as you: same pieces, same speed, same specials. The original game did not have them; they stand in for the other people on the server. Tick a row to add that opponent, or untick all of them to practise alone. Computer opponents are only used offline; online, the seats are filled by the people who joined. Stack is the “Stack Height at Start” for that player.

Sound and Theme

Original: “The Dance of the Spheres” by Davie M Karlsson (Mind Xpander Productions, 1996), the tetrinet.mid that ships with the game. The others are originals made for this web port: Midnight Circuit (indie-disco / French house), Chrome Nights (French electro / night-drive synthwave) and Evergreen Signal (psychedelic jam-rock over techno). All are MIDI, played through a small built-in synthesizer.

The original data\ folder is built in: the block graphics, the ten sound effects and the music. To use a custom theme, select its .tnp file together with its bitmap, .WAV and .mid files. They load from your computer and are never uploaded.

Game Winlist

3 points for the last player or team standing, 2 for the last to lose, 1 for the one before that. Teams are listed with <T>.

In honor of the original. TetriNET was created by St0rmCat in 1997 and became one of the great multiplayer games of the early internet: six players, special blocks, and endless late nights on IRC servers. The game, its design and its name belong to its creator, and every idea you play here is theirs. Thank you, St0rmCat.

About this version. This is a new, independent web port of that original creation, rebuilt so it can be played in a browser and online today. It is not made or endorsed by St0rmCat. The rules follow the original program closely: the seven pieces and their random starting orientation, rotation, drop speed by level, the short delay before a piece locks, soft drop, the pause between pieces, and every special block behave as they did in the original. What's new here: online game rooms with public and private games and invite links, computer opponents for practice, larger opponent fields, the Modern display, and the special-block guide.

Keys: arrows move/rotate/soft‑drop, Ctrl rotates left, Space drops, 1–6 use your first special on that player (your own number for good blocks), D discards it, T talks. P pauses.

Special blocks: A Add Line, C Clear Line, N Nuke Field, R Random Blocks Clear, S Switch Fields, B Clear Special Blocks, G Block Gravity, Q Blockquake, O Block Bomb.

Online play goes through a small WebSocket server (server.js): any number of games, up to six players each, and the first player in is the moderator. Offline, computer opponents take the empty seats.
